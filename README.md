Blood Donor Finder System

Project Description:

Blood Donor Finder System is a web-based application designed to help patients quickly find suitable blood donors during emergencies. The system allows users to register as blood donors by providing details such as name, blood group, location, contact information, and availability. Patients or hospitals can search for donors based on blood group and location, making it easier to connect with potential donors.
The system aims to reduce the time required to find blood during emergencies and create a centralized platform connecting donors, patients, hospitals, and blood banks. Future enhancements can include GPS-based donor location, chatbot assistance, emergency notifications, donor availability status, and secure user authentication.

Problem Statement:

Finding a suitable blood donor during an emergency can be difficult and time-consuming. Traditional methods such as contacting friends, family, or blood banks may not provide immediate results, especially when a specific blood group is required. There is a need for a centralized system that helps patients quickly search for and connect with available blood donors based on blood group and location.

Objectives:

To develop a user-friendly web application for finding blood donors.
To allow donors to register with their blood group, location, and contact details.
To enable patients to search for donors based on required blood group and location.
To reduce the time required to find suitable donors during emergencies.
To connect donors and recipients through a centralized platform.
To maintain donor information in an organized and easily accessible manner.
To provide scope for future features such as GPS-based location, chatbot support, emergency alerts, and donor availability status.
